Now for the real surprise.

Happy 23rd birthday, Priyadarshini! I hope today reminds you just how loved and adored you are — and if it doesn’t, consider this your official reminder. You’re honestly one of those people who are just impossible not to like. After being out of touch for so long, I never imagined I’d end up finding such a great friend in you, but I’m genuinely glad I did. Also, consider this a little reminder to not lose sight of your health while chasing your career and all the things you want to achieve. You’re only 23, and you’ve got a whole life ahead of you, so don’t get so caught up in building your future that you forget to take care of yourself in the present. And of course, that life would be considerably less exciting without me around to entertain you. Jokes apart, I genuinely wish you good health, happiness, success, and plenty of reasons to smile this year. You deserve all the good things life has to offer. Have the happiest birthday!
